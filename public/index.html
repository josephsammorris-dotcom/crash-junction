One hit. Total carnage. The biggest bill wins.

How to play
  1. 1
    Line it upRace across the mesa and down the ridge to a busy crossroads, past traffic both ways. You get 10 seconds of boost: hold it when you need it to time your arrival.
  2. 2
    Make one hitPlough into the traffic. Nobody can stop in time, and the pile-up grows on its own.
  3. 3
    Run up the billScore is damage × vehicles wrecked. Fires, exploding tankers, rollovers and jackknifed semis all add to it.
How many players?

Three runs each, and your best run counts. Pass the controls between turns.
