# Crash Junction audio

Put recorded sounds here and the game uses them automatically, replacing the generated
stand-ins one by one. Use exactly these names; `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav` and `.m4a` all work.
Add `-2`, `-3` (up to `-6`) for variations, picked at random (e.g. `impact-heavy-2.mp3`).

## Music (loop seamlessly; about 2–3 minutes each)
- `menu`: title, turn and final screens
- `drive`: the run, the crash and replays
- `results`: the results screen

## One-off sounds
- `crash`: your big first impact
- `impact-heavy`: big vehicle-on-vehicle hits
- `impact-light`: smaller knocks and scrapes
- `glass`: windscreen and windows shattering
- `explosion`: fuel tanker going up
- `horn-car`, `horn-bus`, `horn-truck`: horns (truck = air horn)
- `airbrake`: hiss of a truck or bus air brake
- `boost`: whoosh when you boost
- `click`: menu button
- `medal`: results jingle

## Loops (seamless, a few seconds long)
- `engine`: your car's engine at a steady rev (pitch follows your speed)
- `skid`: tyre screech
- `scrape`: metal scraping along tarmac
- `fire`: vehicle fire roar and crackle
- `traffic`: distant road traffic
